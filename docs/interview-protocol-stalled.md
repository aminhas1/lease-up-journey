# Short protocol: an applicant who has not gotten through

**Length:** 15 minutes, 20 at most. **For:** someone who has applied and has not moved forward, whichever way that happened. Find out which way in the first two minutes, then follow one branch.

## Say first (1 min)

- I'm learning how the lottery works for the people applying. I'm not with the city or any building, and nothing here touches an application.
- No documents, numbers or account details needed. Describing from memory is enough.
- About 15 minutes. "What happened next" is the main question.

## Where did it stop? (2 min)

- When did you start applying, and roughly how many buildings?
- Has your number ever come up, meaning someone contacted you asking for documents?
  - **No, never contacted:** branch A.
  - **Yes, contacted, but it didn't go through:** branch B. This is the one the research can't see; if you get it, spend the time here.

## Branch A: never reached (8 min)

- How did you first hear about the lottery, and what did you expect to happen?
- How do you decide what to apply to? Do you check the income range and household size on the listing, or apply to everything?
- When did you last update your profile: income, who lives with you, where you live? Is it current right now?
- Has anything about your situation changed since you first applied (new job, pay, household)? Did you change it on Housing Connect?
- Have you gotten any email or text from Housing Connect since applying? What did it say? Did you ever wonder if one was a scam?
- Do you know what happens if your number comes up: what they'd ask for and how long you'd have?
- If it happened tomorrow, what would you have ready, and what would you have to go find?
- Has anyone (a friend, an organization, a counselor) ever helped you with any of this?

*Listen for:* the profile being stale (the snapshot rule freezes it when the number comes up), not knowing the document step exists, notices lost in spam or mistaken for scams, applying outside the income band and never being told, no idea of the 10-weekday window.

## Branch B: reached, didn't get through (8 min)

- How did you find out your number came up: email, text, letter, logging in?
- What did it ask for, and how long did it give you? Did you see it in time?
- What did you do first? What did you manage to gather, and what couldn't you get, or get in time?
- Did you submit anything? Where: Housing Connect, email, in person, another website? Did you hear back that it was received?
- What happened after that: a second request, a rejection, silence? If a rejection, what reason did it give, and did you understand it?
- Did you know you could appeal? Did you?
- What was the moment you stopped, and why that moment?
- Did anyone help, or try to?

*Listen for:* the notice seen late or not at all, the deadline, a document that had to come from someone else, the upload spot not found, submitting once and then being locked out, no receipt, a reason they couldn't parse (income math, deposits, household mismatch), "window expired", not knowing appeals exist, a system message versus a person.

## Both branches: close (3 min)

- If a friend started applying tomorrow, what would you tell them?
- What's the one thing you wish someone had told you?
- Do you know anyone else who applied and didn't get through?
- Thank them. If branch B, ask whether they still have the request or rejection email and would share it with details covered.

## Don't

- Don't explain the rules, even when they're wrong about one. The gap is the finding.
- Don't ask "was it hard to find the upload button". Ask "where did you send it".
- Don't ask their income, rent or log number.
